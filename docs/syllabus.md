Projects:

## Expectations for learner participation and interaction


Course activities will require you to interact with other students in the course.


- Project repositories must maintain proper version control practices
- All team members should contribute actively to projects
- Groups need to maintain communication through designated channels


## Attendance


- Attendance policies will be specified by the instructor
- If you must be absent for a reason sanctioned by the university, contact the instructor before time

## Community guidelines 


- Use a respectful tone in all forms of communication
- Maintain professionalism in your written communication
- Respect regional dialects and culturally embedded ways of oral communication
- Treat each community member with respect
- Respect cultural differences that may influence communication styles


## Disabilities


Reasonable accommodations will be made for students with verifiable disabilities. Contact your institution's disability services office for more information.


## Academic Integrity


Students are required to comply with the university policy on academic integrity. 

Cheating will be punished to the full extent possible. Students are encouraged to collaborate appropriately while maintaining individual academic integrity.


## Student Privacy


- Class sessions may be recorded
- Contact your instructor if you have concerns about privacy


## Syllabus Modification Statement


Our syllabus represents a flexible agreement. It outlines the topics we will cover and the order in which we will cover them. Minor changes in the syllabus can occur if we need to adjust the pace of instruction.
